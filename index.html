02 / THE JOURNEY

FROM OPERATIONS TO SYSTEMS

Four environments. One increasingly connected view of business.

The value is not in having worked in different industries. It is in what each one taught me to see.

2016 — 2020Coppel · Electronics

Customers taught me that technology starts with people.

Leading a retail electronics area meant balancing customer needs, sales goals, inventory, warranties, merchandising and team development. It built the customer-facing and communication layer that technical work alone cannot teach.

What stayed with meListen first. Solve second.
2020Phoenix de México · Materials

Materials taught me that flow is a system.

Warehouse operations exposed the hidden choreography behind production: inbound material, FIFO discipline, inventory control, replenishment and people moving in sync. A bottleneck is rarely isolated; it propagates.

What stayed with meDesign the flow, not only the task.
2021 — 2025Mueblerías López · Manufacturing

Manufacturing taught me that constraints create better decisions.

Translating customer requirements into CNC-ready production meant thinking about dimensions, materials, cost, waste and execution at the same time. A good design is not just correct — it is manufacturable.

What stayed with meA solution has to survive reality.
2026 — Presentml&s México · Enterprise Automation

Enterprise systems taught me how to connect the layers.

SAP GUI, Python, Power Platform and data work gave me the tools to turn operational knowledge into repeatable systems: automate a master-data workflow, structure a document audit, centralize procurement tracking, and make status visible.

What stayed with meComplexity becomes manageable when the process becomes visible.

Electronic Customs Records Audit

A document-heavy manual review was reframed as a classification and evidence problem. The workflow recursively inspects fragmented archives, identifies key documents, cross-checks information and produces a traceable audit output.

PythonOCRBusiness RulesExcel
Challenge

Approach

Classify, deduplicate, extract, cross-check and preserve evidence for human review.

Outcome

Manual inspection became a structured exception-driven review with traceable results.
